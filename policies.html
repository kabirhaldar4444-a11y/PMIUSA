Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Comprehensive Legal Framework for Data Collection, Biometric Verification, Storage & Processing

256-bit Encrypted

Commitment to Privacy: This Privacy Policy governs the data collection, storage, processing, and security practices of PMIUSA. By accessing our portal, submitting an application, or enrolling in programs, you explicitly agree to the collection and handling of your data as defined below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to fulfill educational contracts and verify identity:

  • Contact Information: Full name, verified email address, phone number, physical address, and employer/organization details.
  • KYC & Biometric Verification: Government-issued photo IDs (Passport, Driving License, National ID) and live video snapshot recordings collected during KYC verification to prevent examination fraud.
  • Academic Telemetry: Course completion metrics, quiz scores, portal login timestamps, IP addresses, and browser agent headers.
  • Billing Data: Transaction reference IDs, invoice tokens, and payment status indicators (sensitive credit card credentials are processed directly by certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways and never stored on our servers).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Collected information is processed for specific, authorized purposes:

  • Granting authenticated access to online learning management systems and laboratory simulators.
  • Verifying candidate identity prior to examination scheduling and generating tamper-proof cryptographic credentials.
  • Preventing credential fraud, duplicate registrations, and unauthorized distribution of intellectual property.
  • Transmitting critical administrative communications, syllabus updates, and certification validation inquiries.

3. Data Protection, Storage & Security Controls

PMIUSA employs bank-grade security protocols to protect student data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration:

  • All web traffic and REST APIs are secured using TLS 1.3 encryption with 256-bit cipher keys.
  • Uploaded KYC files are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and stored in segregated, access-controlled cloud containers.
  • Access to internal databases is restricted to verified compliance officers on a strict need-to-know basis via multi-factor authentication (MFA).

4. Your Rights & Data Retention

Students hold the right to review their registered personal details, request corrections of erroneous information, or request an export of their academic transcript. Academic completion and certification records are retained permanently to facilitate lifetime verification by prospective employers and regulatory bodies.
