About PMIUSA

16 years of transformative engineering, BPO excellence, and elite tech talent mentorship.

Our Heritage & Mission

Empowering Careers & Driving Global Business

Founded with a vision to deliver world-class IT and business support services, PMIUSA (Premium Management & IT Services USA) has grown into an international powerhouse headquartered in Gurugram's Electronic City.

Recognizing the widening talent gap in software engineering, cloud infrastructure, and AI, we launched the PMIUSA Academy. Today, our students learn inside live corporate environments, guided by experienced tech leads and supported by our robust network of hiring partners.

Innovation First

We continually evolve our tech stack, processes, and course curricula to match the cutting edge of industry practice.

Unwavering Integrity

Transparent communication, strict data protection standards (GDPR/HIPAA), and dedicated student-first guidance.

Guaranteed Excellence
